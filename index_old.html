Acoustic Sleep Zoning

The interior architecture has been specifically engineered around operational acoustic control. When designing for rigorous professional routines—particularly for those orchestrating 12-hour continuous night shifts (e.g., 7:00 PM to 7:00 AM) who mandate unbroken daytime recovery sleep—spatial zoning is just as critical as structural integrity.

The "Deep Sleep" Sanctuary

In all 5 replica flats, the Master Bedrooms have been pushed to the absolute deepest boundary of the plot (South-West and South-East corners). This achieves three critical layers of acoustic decoupling:

  • Street Isolation: A massive 51 feet of physical architectural mass separates the master beds from the North Main Road, nullifying gate operation noises, tandem parking maneuvers, and morning street traffic.
  • Circulation Decoupling: The Master Bedrooms do not share any walls with the central staircase. The heavy footfall of residents utilizing the stairs is entirely buffered by the central wet core and the secondary bedroom.
  • Plumbing Silence: Because the unified bathroom and kitchen are locked into the central zone near the stairs, no water supply lines or drainage pipes run through the Master Bedroom walls. Flushing or showering in any flat will not disturb the ambient silence of the rear sleep zones. The horizontal ground floor trench is acoustically lagged to protect the Ground Floor resident.
