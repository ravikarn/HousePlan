SEC 02

Executive Summary & Workflow Synthesis

READ → EXTRACT → OPTIMIZE → VALIDATE

This interactive architectural planning package critically analyzes, validates, and optimizes the 12-Page Master Architectural, 3D, Structural & Cost Dossier PDF for a 33'0" × 66'0" (2,178 sq.ft.) North-facing urban residential plot. Because the plot is locked on three boundaries (South, East, and West shared with adjacent private properties) and open only along the 33'0" North public road, the design resolves a challenging geometry through a disciplined 30'0" × 60'0" (1,800 sq.ft.) structural footprint paired with strategic setback and Open-to-Sky (OTS) ventilation shafts.

1. Spatial & Rental Optimization 88.0% Efficiency

Eliminates dead corridors by locating a compact 14'6" × 6'6" (94 sq.ft.) dog-legged staircase core mid-depth on the East bay. Each typical upper floor accommodates two symmetrical 790 sq.ft. 2 BHK apartments (675 sq.ft. carpet area) featuring a generous 15'0" × 14'0" Master Bedroom, 14'0" × 12'6" Second Bedroom, 13'0" × 14'0" Hall, and 10'0" × 7'6" East Kitchen.

2. South Flat Balcony & OTS Solution 0'0" Rear Boundary

Because the South boundary has 0'0" setback against the neighbor's property, projecting cantilevers are legally prohibited. Unit 2 (South Flat) is therefore equipped with an internal recessed 13'6" × 3'0" (40.5 sq.ft.) South OTS Utility Balcony enclosed by a safety grille and sliding UPVC glass doors—delivering daylight, cross-breeze, and utility drying without encroaching on the neighbor.

3. Separation of Water & Sewage 50'0" Clear Gap

Freshwater infrastructure (12,500L Underground Sump + 6" Borewell) is isolated in the auspicious North-East Setback, while sewage digestion (8,000L 3-chamber RCC Septic Tank) sits under the North-West Bike Parking Bay, joined via a straight 150mm UPVC 1:60 sewer line along the 3'0" East corridor with 4 inspection chambers.

SEC 04

Technical Conflict, Regulatory Discrepancy & Missing Data Register

Problem → Impact → Recommended Resolution

Rather than silently glossing over technical contradictions between initial client preferences, urban plot constraints, and statutory building bye-laws, every discrepancy is explicitly documented and resolved below.

CONF-01 · BUILDING HEIGHT NOMENCLATURE RESOLVED VIA DUAL-MODE TOGGLE

Problem: The report filename specifies G+2_Residential_Building_Design_Report.html (3 slabs / 5 units), whereas the finalized Master PDF Dossier evolved into a Stilt + 3 Upper Floors (G+3, 4 slabs, 7 units, 7,400 sq.ft.) package.

Impact: Total built-up area (7,400 vs. 5,600 sq.ft.), FAR (3.40 vs. 2.57), and cost (₹1.22 Cr vs. ₹92.3L) differ by ~24.3%.

Recommended Resolution: Both configurations are fully supported in this report via the top-bar live toggle, and every table clearly states whether figures apply to the 4-slab PDF Master or the 3-slab G+2 variant.

CONF-02 · ZERO-SETBACK BOUNDARIES VS. STATUTORY BYE-LAWS REGULATORY VARIANCE FLAGGED

Problem: The layout builds up to 0'0" on West and South boundaries (82.6% coverage, 3.40 Gross FAR), whereas Bihar Building Bye-Laws 2014 prescribes front 3.0m, rear 1.5m, and FAR 1.5–2.0 for standard plots.

Impact: Zero-boundary walls cannot have external windows and require shared-wall structural mitigation and municipal compounding/row-housing approval.

Recommended Resolution: Provide completely windowless 9" external walls on West & South, draw all light/air from internal OTS shafts (3'6"×8'3" West & 13'6"×3'0" South), and use 12"×24" eccentric strap beams so footings never cross neighbor lines.

CONF-03 · SOUTH FLAT BALCONY ON 0'0" PROPERTY LINE ARCHITECTURALLY RESOLVED

Problem: Unit 1 (North) enjoys a 3'6" projecting balcony over the 6'0" front setback, but Unit 2 (South) abuts the neighbor at 0'0" where an overhanging balcony is illegal.

Impact: Without a balcony, the South 2 BHK rental unit would lack semi-open utility drying space and cross-ventilation in its 13'0" × 14'0" Hall.

Recommended Resolution: Carve out a 13'6" × 3'0" (40.5 sq.ft.) recessed South OTS Utility Balcony within the 30'×60' slab footprint with a 3-track UPVC glass slider and protective MS box grille.

CONF-04 · UNVERIFIED GEOTECHNICAL & MUNICIPAL DATA MANDATORY PRE-CONSTRUCTION TEST

Problem: No geotechnical borehole log, groundwater table depth, or municipal sewer invert level was supplied in the user brief.

Impact: Gangetic alluvial plains in Bihar (Seismic Zone IV) can exhibit high water tables and liquefaction risk, affecting footing size (5'6"×5'6" vs. 6'0"×6'0") and septic soak-pit percolation.

Recommended Resolution: Conduct two 10m–15m Standard Penetration Test (SPT) boreholes prior to excavation to verify the assumed 150 kN/m² Safe Bearing Capacity.

4. Gravity Drainage & Plinth Protection

Finished stilt pavement is set at +1'6" and apartment plinth at +2'0" (600 mm) above the North road crown. Both sanitary effluent (150mm UPVC @ 1:60) and rainwater runoff flow South-to-North by gravity along the 3'0" East setback corridor without sump pumps.

SEC 08

Selection of Recommended Design (Option C — Master Dossier Scheme)

SELECTED FOR FULL 14-SHEET CAD DEVELOPMENT

Option C is selected as the recommended architectural solution because it uniquely satisfies every mandatory user requirement without sacrificing room proportions or rental marketability. By placing the 14'6" × 6'6" dog-legged staircase at the mid-point of the East bay (Y = 26'9" to 33'3"), the landing feeds directly into the 13'0" × 14'0" Living/Dining Halls of both North and South apartments with zero corridor loss. Consolidating each apartment's wet zone into one 8'0" × 4'6" Common Bathroom paired with an outside 3'6" × 4'6" Washbasin Niche eliminates dual-toilet clutter, freeing up space for a 210 sq.ft. (15'0" × 14'0") Master Bedroom and an L-shaped 175 sq.ft. (14'0" × 12'6") Second Bedroom ventilated via the central West OTS shaft.

SEC 30

Project Risks & Engineering Mitigations

  • 1. Neighbor Foundation Undercutting Risk: Excavating 6'0" deep along the 0'0" West and South property lines can destabilize shallow adjacent walls. Mitigation: Excavate in alternate pit segments with timber/steel shoring and pour eccentric footings with 12"×24" strap beams immediately.
  • 2. High Monsoon Water Table: Bihar's alluvial plains experience elevated water tables during July–September. Mitigation: Schedule foundation and underground tank (6'6" deep Sump & Septic) works between November and May, using integral crystalline waterproofing.
  • 3. Zero-Boundary Seepage Risk: External plastering on the outside face of West/South walls may be restricted above neighbor roofs. Mitigation: Apply two-coat polymer-modified cementitious waterproofing on outer brick lifts as masonry rises.
